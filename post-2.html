How to Compress Photo and Signature Images for GDS Online Apply and IBPS Forms

Struggling with "file size too large" on GDS Online or IBPS? Learn how to compress your photo and signature to the required KB limit, free.

gds online apply gds online ibps jpg image compressor photo compressor image resizer

If you're applying through GDS Online or filling an IBPS exam form, you've probably hit the same wall: the portal rejects your photo or signature because it's a few KB too big. Application portals are strict, and if you miss the limit by even a single byte, the upload fails.

Always check the official notification first

Size and dimension requirements differ between recruitment cycles and even between fields (photo, signature, thumb impression). Before compressing, read the current official notification and note the exact limit for each file.

Why phone photos get rejected

A modern phone photo can be several MB. Portals often want it in the tens of KB. Simply lowering quality often leaves the file too big, or makes your face and signature look muddy.

Compress to the exact limit

  1. Take or select your photo or signature scan.
  2. Open photoscale and load the image.
  3. Enter the limit from the notification (for example, 50 KB) as a custom size.
  4. Save the result as JPG and upload it.

photoscale applies a built-in 2% safety margin, so the output lands just under your ceiling rather than right on the edge. That matters when a portal rejects anything above the limit.

Tips for GDS and IBPS applicants

  • Keep your original file, and compress a copy.
  • Use a plain background and good lighting so the compressed photo stays clear.
  • Scan signatures on white paper with dark ink. High contrast survives compression best.
  • If a portal also requires specific pixel dimensions, use an image resizer for that step, then compress.

Privacy matters here

These forms involve personal photos and IDs. photoscale processes files in your browser, so nothing is uploaded to a third-party server.
