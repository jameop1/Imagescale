FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I compress an image to an exact size like under 100 KB or 50 KB? ▸
To compress an image to an exact target:
  1. Drop your screenshot, photo, or document scan onto the ingestion area, or paste it directly from your clipboard using Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V).
  2. Select your target ceiling preset (such as 50 KB for official visa and passport portals, or 100 KB for job application ATS uploaders), or type an exact custom limit.
  3. The dual-axis compression algorithm runs in volatile browser memory, iteratively balancing JPEG/WebP quantization and proportional dimension scaling in milliseconds.
  4. Inspect the result with the interactive split-screen comparison lens, then click SAVE to export the file with its proper .jpg or .webp extension.
Why do government, visa, and job application portals reject my photo uploads? ▸
Government visa portals (e.g. US Visa DS-160, Schengen visa portals, passport renewal systems) and corporate applicant tracking systems (ATS) enforce rigid file size ceilings—typically under 100 KB, 50 KB, or 20 KB. If an uploaded image exceeds the threshold by even a single byte (e.g. 100.1 KB), automated gatekeepers reject the submission. ExactSize solves this by applying an automated -2% safety boundary margin, guaranteeing the output file lands safely below the ceiling every time.
Is it safe to compress sensitive documents, ID cards, and passports here? ▸
Yes, 100% safe and confidential. Unlike traditional online image compressors, ExactSize uses client-side HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files are decoded and processed exclusively inside your computer's RAM. Zero bytes are transmitted over the internet, zero copies are cached on external servers, and zero metadata is harvested. You can disconnect your internet connection entirely and the tool will continue to work seamlessly.
How does ExactSize shrink images to exact sizes without making text blurry? ▸
Traditional tools only reduce image encoding quality, which causes severe blocking artifacts and renders text on receipts, IDs, and screenshots illegible. ExactSize employs a dual-axis binary search: it first searches for optimal quality at native resolution. If the target ceiling cannot be achieved at high clarity, it executes proportional geometric scaling based on the exact square-root area ratio. This preserves sharp edges and high-contrast micro-typography while achieving the exact target file size.
How do I compress screenshots or photos for Discord without Nitro? ▸
Free Discord accounts enforce a strict 8 MB or 10 MB file upload limit. When capturing high-resolution 4K gameplay screenshots or raw camera photos, files often exceed 15 MB. Drop your file into ExactSize and click the 8 MB (Discord Nitro) preset. The tool will compress your image just below 8 MB while maintaining virtually lossless visual fidelity.
Why did the browser request permissions or diagnostic checks? ▸
When you click "Copy to Clipboard", modern browsers require user authorization for the Clipboard API to write binary image data into your operating system clipboard. The integrated System Diagnostics & Permissions Audit tool performs an offline verification check to confirm that your browser sandbox, hardware 2D canvas acceleration, and clipboard API are operating with optimal throughput.
